## Models and local inference

**Google's eighth-gen TPU lineup is now four systems: 8tx for confidential inference, 8i Max for million-token context.** (Event date: Monday, Nov 9, 2026 — announced ~7:50 AM ET)
Following up on the 8t (training) and 8i (inference) systems from Google Cloud Next in April, Chief Technologist Amin Vahdat announced two new eighth-generation configurations — the first time Google has introduced four TPU systems in a single year. TPU 8tx reuses 8t silicon paired with 5th-generation AMD EPYC CPU hosts and adds hardware-level confidential compute (data and model weights stay encrypted during processing), adopting the low-latency Boardfly network for up to 2x lower latency than 8t with near-zero performance penalty — aimed at regulated enterprises fine-tuning, distilling and running agents on sensitive data. TPU 8i Max puts two 8i chips in a single package for double the compute, double the SRAM and double the HBM bandwidth at the same rack footprint, targeting the "memory wall" of multi-agent, long-context workloads. Bloomberg's Dina Bass broke the story, noting both systems launch in 2027; Google also took the opportunity to declare GKE Cloud Pathways and GKE dynamic slicing (5x faster job startup, 4.5x faster fault recovery) generally available, and cited "nearly 100" TPU customers.
*Why it matters:* Four TPU variants in twelve months signals Google reconfiguring systems around customer-specific demand — faster than any merchant-silicon cadence — and the AMD EPYC pairing for the confidential-compute tier is a notable crack in the all-Intel host tradition. For the agentic era, Google is explicitly betting that memory bandwidth, not raw FLOPs, is the constraint.
*Local inference note:* none — this is hyperscaler silicon; the practical read for local operators is competitive (TPU price-performance pressure on GPU cloud rental economics).

**Disney names Adobe its "Official AI Innovation Partner" in a multi-year alliance built on custom Firefly Foundry models.** (Event date: Monday, Nov 9, 2026 — announced at Adobe MAX, Miami Beach; The Verge 10:50 AM ET)
Adobe will build proprietary generative models trained exclusively on Disney's own properties — character attributes, environments, visual styles — via Firefly Foundry, which Adobe pitches as "human-centered and artist-led," with commercially safe, production-ready outputs. The deal covers Walt Disney World, Disneyland, Disney Cruise Line, ESPN Edge and the studios' StudioLAB, adds dedicated Adobe engineering/research resources, and extends the June Imagineering collaboration in which Firefly Foundry already turns hand-drawn concept sketches into rendered 2D concept art. *Why it matters:* This is the template for how legacy IP holders will consume generative AI: not off-the-shelf models, but closed, licensed, on-brand model families co-built with a vendor who brings deployment muscle. Expect every IP-rich media company to study this deal — and every stock-AI shop to worry.

It reframes the board investigation from personal-crisis to corporate-accountability story.

**EU's draft Digital Fairness Act would force adult opt-outs for addictive features and take aim at AI companion bots.** (Event date: Monday, Nov 9, 2026 — Bloomberg 10:40 AM ET; Reuters wire 11:21 AM ET)
Per the leaked draft, companies would be required to let adult users disable features that encourage excessive use, and the law explicitly targets AI systems that simulate personal relationships; penalties would run up to 6% of global annual turnover — the DSA-style enforcement level. The draft lands while Brussels' separate "Kids Act" push for safe-by-design standards proceeds, on top of existing DSA investigations into Meta and TikTok over addictive design.
*Why it matters:* Regulating "compulsive engagement" architectures is the next frontier after content moderation — it reaches into model behavior and product design (companion chatbots, infinite feeds) rather than just takedown duties. For AI companies, a 6%-of-revenue compliance risk on design choices will shape EU product roadmaps years before enforcement.
