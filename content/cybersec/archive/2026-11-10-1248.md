Event date: November 10, 2026 (status-page update posted 17:12 UTC / 12:12 PM ET). N-able's consolidated incident post (ID 202580, Major Outage, started Nov 9 14:00 UTC) now says it is "observing scanning activity associated with the published proof-of-concept code" for Able2Shell — the 33-zero-day, four-chain disclosure from an independent researcher (no CVEs assigned; vendor rates the set critical; CVSS scores not specified by source) — while maintaining "at this time, we have no evidence that any customer environment has been successfully exploited." Any org that quietly re-onlines an unpatched server after the outage will be hit.
**Action:** Keep hosted and on-prem N-central offline until N-able's hotfix lands and its status page explicitly clears bring-back-online (per vendor instructions); hunt for exploitation attempts in perimeter logs for the published PoC SOAP/gzip/MMS3 traffic patterns now.

## Attacks and campaigns

**Huntress: patient AhsayCBS operator turns backup servers into long-term footholds — PNG-stego loader, host-bound Python implant, Veeam-credential-theft command** — *Lead*
Event date: November 9–10, 2026 (Huntress follow-up report dated Nov 9; The Hacker News write-up Nov 10, 11:00 AM ET). Analyzing three AhsayCBS servers (Ahsay's backup-management console) exploited October 8–11 via the CVE-2026-105133 (Medium, auth bypass in `checkSysPwd`) → CVE-2026-105134 (Critical, unauthenticated RCE as `NT AUTHORITY\SYSTEM` via `/rps/api/json/UpdateReceivers.do`) chain, Huntress recovered far more than the October smash-and-grab webshells and XMRig it first reported: five distinct JSP webshell families (keyed shells that 404 to scanners, shells with arbitrary file-write, self-deleting `status_`/`st_` shells), `nsmind.jsp` (a signed file-transfer API with exec/cd/upload/download), and `AhsayCBS.jsp`, which decrypts its payload from the least-significant bits of PNG pixels and executes it from memory. A second XMRig miner (`kvm-agent.exe`, MSR access enabled) re-launches every minute from a SYSTEM scheduled task with different wallet/pools; a Python implant runs only when a marker file matching the SHA-256 of the host's `MachineGuid` exists, reflectively loading an x64 DLL that phones `creepexplosiondarknight[.]com` over HTTPS; gost/chisel tunnels, AnyDesk/Atera/Splashtop, and broad Defender exclusions completed the kit — plus a command consistent with NetExec's Veeam credential-theft module. CVSS numbers not specified by source; hotfixes 10.3.4.45 (v10) and 9.15.2.70 (v7–v9) close the entry point, and Huntress verified 10.3.4.45 blocks the exploit.
**Why it matters:** This is backup infrastructure as deliberate, persistent espionage/cyber-ready staging — the operator evicted nothing, just entrenched, and probed Veeam credentials on a related server. Patching the CVE does not evict any of it: every AhsayCBS server exposed pre-hotfix should be treated as potentially shelld.
**Action:** Hunt the listed artifacts (webshell names/parameters, Run-key Python installs under the SYSTEM profile, scheduled-task miner, MachineGuid-named marker files, gost/chisel/AnyDesk/Atera/Splashtop binaries, Defender exclusions) on any AhsayCBS host that was internet-exposed before mid-October hotfixing; apply 10.3.4.45/9.15.2.70 and rotate everything those servers could see.

## Defensive tools and advisories

**Google opens Gemini 4 Carbon to paying developers; DeepMind formally proves libsignal with ~6.5M lines of AI-generated Lean code** — *Update on previously reported* (gemini-4-carbon, footnoted Oct 28)
Event date: November 10, 2026 (SecurityWeek, 11:42 AM ET; announcement "on Tuesday"). Carbon — previously exclusive to vetted Fairwind-program defenders — is now on the Gemini API, Gemini for Business, and AI Ultra, at introductory $2/$10 per million input/output tokens through November 30. Google says Wiz used Carbon-for-cyber to find zero-days at two major SaaS providers within two hours, and its Cloud Vulnerability Research team confirmed two bugs in a popular open-source project were exploitable at severity "comparable to PHP2Shell," prompting a critical re-classification and patch. The headline result: DeepMind's AlphaProof Code paired five researchers with Carbon- and Claude-Opus-5.5-driven agents to write specifications and machine-generate ~6.5 million lines of Lean proofs for libsignal — proving forward secrecy, post-compromise security and authentication at the level of compiled machine code (most prior work verifies design only), in four weeks instead of years; re-injected historical bugs broke the proofs as they should. Google is publicly releasing the specifications and proofs (caveats: Google's own build; side channels partly out of scope).
**Why it matters:** Two shifts at once — a frontier cyber model moves from gated defender access to commercial availability, and formal verification of the messaging stack under WhatsApp/Google Messages just went from specialist-years to team-weeks, which is the strongest evidence yet that AI closes the verification-cost gap.
Source:
- SecurityWeek — "Google Widens Gemini 4 Carbon Access After AI Agents Verify Signal Code" (Eduard Kovacs), Nov 10, 2026 11:42 AM ET — https://www.securityweek.com/google-widens-gemini-4-carbon-access-after-ai-agents-verify-signal-code/

**WhiteMatter launches from stealth with $20M Series A for per-agent "guardian" governance**
Event date: November 10, 2026 (PR Newswire 09:00 ET; SecurityWeek 09:58 ET). The startup deploys a dedicated guardian agent for every AI agent in an environment, aiming for continuous governance of agents, models, tools and integrations as autonomous systems multiply unvetted inside enterprises.
**Why it matters:** This is the governance-layer land-grab for the agentic era — expect the runtime-policy vendors (Sysdig, CrowdStrike, Google) to be its direct competitive set; useful signal for where agent-oversight budgets go in FY27.
