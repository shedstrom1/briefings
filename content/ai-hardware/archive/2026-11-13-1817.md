## Inference software and benchmarks

**Tandem-vLLM 0.2.0: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on 3090/4090-class, A6000, Ada and Hopper — with a two-GPU "expert helper" mode** (event date: November 13, announced 16:03 ET)
Tandem-vLLM is an expert-offload build of vLLM 0.31 for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash whose routed experts live in host RAM as 2-bit vector codes (2.1 bits/weight) with a GPU cache of hot experts; cache-miss experts are computed on the CPU in parallel with the GPU, so nothing is skipped and output is exact for the package's weights (293 GB VQ2 package, ~300 GB disk, 128 GB+ RAM, 256 GB to pin). Version 0.2.0 widens support from Blackwell-only to Ampere (RTX 30xx, A6000), Ada (RTX 40xx, RTX 6000 Ada) and Hopper (H100/H200), and adds two-GPU operation two ways: tensor-parallel (best with a strong CPU) or a "helper GPU" (HELPER_GPU=1) where the second card caches and computes hot experts so the CPU does less — no P2P or NVLink required. Reported numbers: 75.5 tok/s single-user decode (93.1 tensor-parallel) on 2× RTX PRO 6000 96 GB with an EPYC 9534; 16.8 tok/s one-GPU / 21.7 helper on the 24 GB shape; 60.7 tok/s on H200 (Threadripper PRO 7965WX); 414 tok/s aggregate at 32 streams on one RTX PRO 6000. Quality vs the official FP4 checkpoint: GPQA-diamond 76.8% vs 81.3% at pure 2-bit experts, recovering roughly half the gap (FP4_TOP=52 keeps the 52 most-routed experts per layer in FP4); GSM8K 194 vs 192; passkey intact to 94k. A sibling fork, tandem.cpp (llama.cpp patch), targets 16 GB cards and possibly 12 GB with 64 GB+ RAM — 16 GB cards cannot be the main GPU in the vLLM build. A commenter compared it to FreeToken; the author accepts the lineage but notes it is DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-specific and vLLM-based, with stock OpenAI-compatible server, continuous batching, prefix caching, tool calling, image input and DSpark spec-decode.
- *Why it matters:* this is the first credible way to run a 748B-class Flash-Next MoE on cards people already own — Ampere 3090s and A6000s included — and the helper-GPU trick turns a weak-CPU box's biggest bottleneck (CPU expert compute) into a cheap second GPU problem. Exactness (nothing approximated away) plus vLLM serving features puts serious pressure on Strata/Basalt's homelab turf.
- *Local inference:* 24 GB minimum main GPU (3090/4090/A6000-class), 128–256 GB DDR5 host RAM, Linux + CUDA 13, AVX2+; H100/H200/RTX PRO 6000 for the fast lane.

**SGLang pushes the rust-server to parity: ~15 hardening PRs, FA3 moves to its own wheel, sgl-kernel 0.4.11** (event date: November 13)
The rust frontend absorbed a cluster: streamed /generate error typing/retryability (#49663), 503-instead-of-block on a full tokenizer queue (#49635), --warmups/--gc-warning-threshold honoring (#50189), named abort hooks (#50190), multimodal processor fail-once semantics (#49907), SHA-256 CPU instructions on aarch64 (#50204), /v1/loads snapshot-lock fix (#49635); HiCache now stages kernel-backend host index uploads in pinned memory (#49633) and PD-disaggregation top-logprobs width is configurable (#49686); FA3 is served from the standalone sgl-fa3 wheel and dropped from sglang-kernel (#50260), with sgl-kernel bumped to 0.4.11 (#50369).
- *Why it matters:* the Rust frontend takes tokenizer/scheduler work off the Python GIL — the usual CPU-side bottleneck on single-socket inference boxes — and the FA3 wheel split finally makes attention kernels independently updatable.
- *Local inference:* lower CPU overhead on 8–16-core homelab servers; DGX Spark/ARM (aarch64 SHA-256) benefits directly.

## Community spins and quantizations

**Nace AI ships "weight-space quantized" GGUFs — agents edit tensor precisions, KL is the reward (up to 2x better KL claimed)** (event date: November 13, r/LocalLLaMA announcement 14:23 ET; update on the Nov 12 "KQuant-MLX-Q4" ledger entry)
Alish from nace.ai (the lab behind the top-ranked Artificial Analysis decision model and Kapitsa Apple-Silicon inference) released GGUFs of Qwen3.5-9B, Qwen3.8-27B, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next built by Weight Space Programming: after Unsloth-style candidate-mix search plateaued at SOTA parity, Astra/Fable/Carbon agents in Nace's WSP harness proposed per-tensor precision changes, each kept or reverted on measured KL divergence against held-out post-October-2026 English Wikipedia, chat/tool-call and GitHub-code text (512 → 128K tokens). Claims: best-in-class KL among 50+ 9B quants at the Q3 tier (beating Unsloth and EXL3 references), holds up scaling to 27B/35B, weaker on Flash-Next; roughly one billion tokens of agent compute burned; a paper is promised and the harness is currently closed-source ("open-sourcing the harness app" pending). Weight uploads ran from Nov 12 ~17:07 ET (9B/35B) through Nov 13 02:38 ET (27B), with the Flash-Next package uploaded Nov 12 ~21:32 ET. Treat all figures as author-run: the same lab's MLX KQuant claims last week also lacked third-party replication.
- *Why it matters:* this is the first production use of the weight-space-programming wave (MIT's Nov 2 demo, last week's Tree-Ising and DSPy work) to beat static quantization algorithms rather than just match them — if replicated, quantization becomes an agentic optimization loop, and the "which quant has the best bits where" arms race gets a new axis.
- *Local inference:* drop-in GGUF replacements at ~4-bit tiers — 9B (~5 GB), 35B-A3B (~21 GB, 16 GB cards with light offload), 27B Q4 (~16-17 GB, the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB class), Flash-Next for Strata/Basalt setups.
- Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1xvh4ag/weight_space_programming_for_qua (Nov 13, 14:23 ET)
- Source: https://huggingface.co/nace-ai/Qwen3.8-27B-GGUF (created Nov 13, 02:38 ET) + /nace-ai/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GGUF, /nace-ai/Qwen3.5-9B-GGUF, /nace-ai/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-GGUF (Nov 12–13)

**Intel's November ISA reference formally confirms Razor Lake and Coral Rapids model IDs** (event date: November 13, published 15:51 ET)
Intel's Architecture ISA Extensions Programming Reference rev 063 adds Razor Lake desktop (12_06H, 12_0AH) and laptop (12_07H, 12_09H) model values under family 18, alongside Coral Rapids Xeon (13_08H) — with GCC patches this week indicating Razor Lake carries Nova Lake's full ISA (AVX10.2 + APX), and Coral Rapids adding VPMLDSABFLYDQ (hardware assist for the ML-DSA post-quantum signature scheme) while keeping SMT on track for 2028.
- *Why it matters:* AVX10.2/APX continuity confirms the instruction target community CPU-inference kernels (llama.cpp ggml, KTransformers prefill paths) will optimize against for the next two x86 generations; the spec also dates the client (2028, LGA-1954) and server windows.
- *Local inference:* CPU-side prefill/offload builds on future Intel desktops keep AVX10.2 — no new ISA risk for GGML; nothing purchasable today.
- Source: https://www.techpowerup.com/354804/intels-isa-reference-confirms-razor-lake-desktop-and-laptop-model-ids (Nov 13)
